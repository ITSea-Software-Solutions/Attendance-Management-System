Thank you for your time. We are pleased to present TrueCrew — a biometric attendance and wage system built specifically for contract and third-party labour, where workers belong to multiple contractors, move between sites, and are paid by days and hours rather than a fixed salary.

TrueCrew is delivered as a fully hosted service — we run the servers, your team simply signs in. It uses the computers you already own at the gate, keeps working when the internet drops, and produces the attendance and the wage register your payroll needs — in Excel.

1. What TrueCrew does

Biometric gate attendanceFingerprint IN/OUT with an automatic photo of the person at every punch. Nobody can mark anyone else present.
Up to three fingersHeavy work wears prints down — a backup and third finger are enrolled, and any one of them opens the gate.
Face attendance tooAny webcam or phone camera works as a second method, verified on the server.
Works offlineThe gate keeps running when the internet drops; punches sync automatically the moment it returns.
Hands-free modeThe scanner stays armed — a worker places a finger and the mark happens by itself, no taps from the guard.
Live boardReal-time who-is-inside by gate and department, for the security cabin or HR screen.

Built for contractor-supplied labour

  • Every contractor manages their own workers from a mobile app — they register, enroll and deploy their people; you never do their data entry.
  • Nobody enters your plant without your approval. Each deployment needs your HR's sign-off, with the department and gate they are allowed to use.
  • Aadhaar-verified identity for every worker, with the number stored masked and the same person blocked from being registered twice across different contractors.
  • Contractor-wise reporting — days, hours, overtime and payable split per contractor, so every bill you receive can be checked against the gate record.

Visitors and gate passes

Gate passes with the guest's live photo, sent to the host employee for approval on WhatsApp; entry is only allowed once the host says yes. Entry and exit are stamped, and every decision is recorded.

2. Payroll & wage register INCLUDED

Attendance is only useful if it turns into money correctly. TrueCrew includes a full wage register, so the same system that records the punch also produces the payment sheet:

  • Pay cycles that match site practice — 26th-to-25th, 21st-to-20th, 16th-to-15th or calendar month, with one-click stepping between cycles.
  • Wage master per worker — set the monthly rate once; the day rate (rate ÷ 26) and the overtime rate (day rate ÷ 8) follow automatically. Rates are edited on screen and saved in bulk. Every divisor is configurable to your policy.
  • The muster sheet you already know — a worker × day grid with P / A / WO / H codes and the daily overtime row beneath, ending in OT hours, OT amount, total days, rate, arrears and payable amount. Downloads straight to Excel.
  • Overtime from real punches — computed from actual IN/OUT times rather than typed in. A day worked on a weekly-off or holiday counts entirely as overtime, and any manual overtime records who approved it and when.
  • Weekly-off and holiday calendars, so an off day is never counted as an absence.
  • Arrears, advances, deductions and bonuses applied per worker per cycle.
  • Exceptions caught before you pay — workers with no rate set, a missing OUT, overtime on an absent day, work on an off day or unapproved overtime are all listed on one strip, so mistakes are found before the money leaves.
The result: your muster and wage register are generated from biometric facts, in the Excel format your team already uses — with no manual counting, no retyping, and no formula that can quietly go wrong.
