1. What you get

  • Aadhaar-verified worker registry — every worker registered with identity documents and fingerprint; your existing worker list is imported from your Excel sheet on day one (Aadhaar verification can follow later, tracked in the system).
  • Biometric gate attendance — fingerprint IN/OUT with an automatic photo of the person at every mark; works fully offline and syncs on its own. Hands-free mode: worker just places a thumb.
  • Live Board — real-time display of who is inside, per gate and department, for your security cabin / HR office TV.
  • Contractor (vendor) control — each contractor manages their own workers from a mobile app; every deployment to your plant requires your HR's approval with department/gate assignment.
  • Visitor gate passes — guest photo at the gate; the host employee approves on WhatsApp (or by phone, recorded) before entry is allowed.
  • Reports — daily and monthly attendance in Excel (CSV) and printable formats, photo-verified, for wage and contractor-bill verification.
  • Roles for your team — admin, HR (approvals + manual corrections) and gate operators, each seeing only what they need. Every action is audit-logged.
