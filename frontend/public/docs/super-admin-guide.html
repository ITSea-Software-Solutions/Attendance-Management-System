Super Admin Guide

Run the TrueCrew platform: organisations, plans, users, and oversight

Platform v1.10.0 Apps v0.9.34 Updated 22 Aug 2026

1. What TrueCrew is

TrueCrew is a workforce attendance platform for sites that use contract labor. Vendor companies register their workers with an Aadhaar-verified identity and a fingerprint. Companies approve those vendors and receive the workers. At the gate, each worker marks IN and OUT with a fingerprint or face scan. There is no paper register and no proxy attendance — every record is tied to a verified person, a time, and a gate.

The whole system in four steps:

  • 1Vendors register workers Each worker is enrolled with their Aadhaar (verified identity) and a fingerprint. A photo enables face attendance too.
  • 2Vendors deploy workers to companies A deployment says which worker goes to which company, and for which dates. It waits for the company's approval by default, and can be limited to specific gates.
  • 3Workers mark IN / OUT at the gate A fingerprint or face scan identifies the worker in seconds — on the web gate or on the Android / Windows apps, even offline.
  • 4Everyone sees role-scoped attendance Companies see attendance at their site. Vendors see their own workers. You, the Super Admin, see everything.

2. Your role

You are the platform owner. Nothing on TrueCrew is hidden from you. Every company, vendor, worker, attendance record, and plan is visible in your portal.

What only the Super Admin can do:

  • Create organisations — set up companies and vendors, each with their first admin login.
  • Manage all users — create, edit, or disable any user account on the platform, in any organisation.
  • Control subscriptions — approve or reject upgrade requests, or set any organisation's plan directly.
  • Edit global notification templates — the default texts every organisation starts with.
  • Override safety locks — for example, remove a worker that a vendor cannot (see Worker lifecycle rules).
  • Watch everything — dashboards, attendance views, exports, and the audit log across all organisations.
Day-to-day work belongs to the organisations. Companies approve vendors and deployments; vendors register and deploy workers. Your job is to set organisations up, keep plans in order, and step in when something needs an override.

3. Organisations

There are two kinds of organisations. A company runs a site and receives workers. A vendor supplies the workers. Organisations reach TrueCrew in three ways — you create them, they sign up themselves, or a company creates its own vendor.

Create a company (Companies page)

  1. Open Companies and click Add Company. Fill in the name, address, and contact details. GST is optional.
  2. Create the Company Admin login. Enter the admin's name and email, then generate a password. The password is shown only once — copy it before saving.
  3. Share the credentials. Send the login email and password to the Company Admin and confirm they can sign in.

Create a vendor (Vendors page)

Your Vendors page is the global list — every vendor on the platform. Creating a vendor works the same way as a company: fill in the details, create the Vendor Admin login, and share the credentials.

Self-signup

Anyone can sign up at /signup as a Company or a Vendor. New organisations start on the free Trial plan. If they pick a paid plan during signup, an upgrade request is filed for you to approve (see Subscriptions & plans).

Company admins can create their own vendors. A company can add a vendor it already works with. That vendor is auto-approved for the company and inherits the company's plan. You will see these vendors on your global Vendors page like any other.

Vendor detail & sharing consent (new in v1.8)

Companies can click any vendor in their list to open a vendor detail view — the organisation's profile and contacts, the relationship timeline, the workers and deployments that vendor has with them, and the vendor's attendance history at their site.

  • Consent is collected on the access request. When a vendor asks for access to a company, it explicitly consents to sharing these details. Vendors a company creates itself consent implicitly.
  • No consent, no details. Without it, the company sees only the vendor's name and the request status.

How payment works (offline)

TrueCrew does not take payment online. Money changes hands outside the system — bank transfer, UPI, cheque, whatever you agree. The portal just tracks the request and the decision:

  1. The organisation requests an upgrade. Their admin opens Plan & Billing and files an upgrade request (or picks a paid plan at signup).
  2. You see the request on Subscriptions. The row shows the organisation, its current plan, its usage, and the requested plan.
  3. You settle payment offline, then decide. Approve to apply the new plan at once, or reject with a note. The organisation is notified either way.
You can also set any plan directly. No request needed — pick the organisation on Subscriptions and set its plan. Useful for partners, demos, or corrections.
Fairness rule: only workers who actually worked count. An organisation's "workers" usage counts a worker only once they have been deployed and marked IN at least once. Registering workers is free. Importing hundreds by CSV is free. The quota is spent when a worker actually starts working.

6. Deployment approvals & permissions

This is a company-side feature, but you should know how it works — it changes who can pass a gate.

Approval is required by default (since v1.7) for every company: each new vendor deployment waits for the company's HR or admin to review it. A company that prefers instant deployments can switch the requirement off in its approvals panel. While approval is required:

  • Each new deployment waits in a pending queue for the company's HR or admin.
  • A pending worker cannot mark attendance anywhere at that company until approved.
  • Rejections carry a reason back to the vendor.
  • On approval, the company can restrict the worker to specific gates or departments. The rule is enforced everywhere — web gate, both apps (even offline), and on the server for every synced mark.
Your own deployments skip the queue. A deployment you create yourself as Super Admin is approved automatically — handy for demos and corrections. Vendor deployments always follow the company's setting.

Preset departments

Wherever departments are picked, a standard list is offered. Main Gate is preselected, so workers can always enter and exit at the main gate unless HR says otherwise. Custom names are also allowed, and an "All gates" option clears the restriction.

Main Gate (default) Back Gate Security HR Production Quality Stores & Warehouse Maintenance Dispatch Canteen Admin Office
Both sides are notified. Company admins get a notification when a deployment needs approval; the vendor gets the decision. Gate devices pick up approvals automatically within about 90 seconds.

7. Worker lifecycle rules

Status flow

StatusMeaning
PendingRegistered, but no fingerprint enrolled yet. Cannot mark attendance.
ActiveFingerprint enrolled. Ready to be deployed and to mark attendance.
InactiveManually disabled. Cannot mark attendance until reactivated.

Aadhaar is mandatory

  • Every worker is registered with Aadhaar — either by importing the e-Aadhaar PDF (details auto-fill) or by typing the 12-digit number.
  • The raw Aadhaar number is never stored. TrueCrew keeps only the masked last four digits and a secure one-way code used for duplicate checks.
  • Duplicates are rejected across all vendors. The same Aadhaar cannot be registered twice on the platform, even by different vendors.

The engagement lock (new in v1.6.1)

A working worker is protected. Once a worker is deployed to a company — or is currently checked IN — the vendor cannot delete the worker, deactivate them, or remove their fingerprint. The deployment must be cancelled first. This stops a vendor from silently pulling a worker's identity out from under a company mid-engagement.
  • To remove a worker, the vendor cancels the deployment first (the worker must be checked OUT), then deletes.
  • You, the Super Admin, can override the lock when there is a genuine reason.
  • Deleting a worker never deletes their attendance. The history stays for company records and audits.

8. Photo-verified attendance

Since v1.7, an attendance mark is more than a time — it is a picture. The moment a fingerprint or face scan verifies at the gate, the device quietly photographs the person and saves the capture with that IN / OUT record. This works on Android and Windows gate machines alike, and it is best-effort: a missing camera never blocks a mark.

Where the photos show up

  • Attendance Log — every row carries the day's live gate photo (falling back to the registration photo). Click the row for the day detail: the registration photo, the Aadhaar photo, and the gate IN and OUT captures side by side, with times, gates, methods, and match scores.
  • Worker analytics — every recent mark shows its own gate capture and exactly how it was verified: thumb (fingerprint + score), photo (face + score), or manual.

The automatic face cross-check

Every gate capture is automatically compared against the worker's enrolled face. A capture that does not match is flagged in the day detail. The flag is advisory — the mark still stands — but it is the buddy-punching alarm: if worker A scanned and worker B stood at the camera, the mismatch shows.

Identification is conservative, too. When two different workers score too close together on a fingerprint or face scan, the scan is rejected and retried rather than guessed — the system never picks a winner from an ambiguous match.

9. Visitors & gate passes

New in v1.10, TrueCrew handles the other people at the gate — guests, couriers, candidates — with the same discipline as workers: a photo, a named approver, and a full decision trail. No visitor enters without an approval on record.

  1. HR maintains the Hosts list. On the Visitors page, the company's HR keeps the list of people allowed to receive visitors — name, mobile, position, department. Only listed hosts can be picked at the gate.
  2. The gate creates the pass. From the gate app's Visitors tab, the gate user enters the guest's name and phone, takes a live photo, and picks the host the guest came to meet — done in seconds.
  3. The host decides. Where the organisation's WhatsApp Business connection is configured, the host is asked on WhatsApp and replies YES or NO. Otherwise — or when the host answers by phone — the gate records the decision manually with a mandatory note, and it is audited.
  4. Entry only after approval. The pass moves waiting → approved (or denied) → inside → left, with entry and exit times stamped along the way.

The portal's Visitors page shows the company's passes — guest photos, status, the WhatsApp or manual decision trail, and entry / exit times.

WhatsApp is optional. The reply-to-approve loop activates once the organisation's WhatsApp Business connection is set up. Until then, manual decisions with the audited note keep gates moving — nothing is blocked.

12. Apps & downloads

TrueCrew ships two apps alongside the web portal — an Android app (APK) and a Windows app (zip). Both are on the Downloads page in the portal, together with the user documents.

What the apps do that the browser cannot:

  • Offline worker registration — details, Aadhaar PDF import, fingerprint, and photo, all without a connection. Everything uploads when the device is back online.
  • Offline fingerprint matching at the gate — enrolled fingerprints sync to gate devices, and every scan is matched on the device itself. The gate keeps working when the internet does not.
  • Photos and proof — a gate photo is taken automatically the moment a scan verifies, and saved with that IN / OUT record as tamper-evident proof — on Android and, since v0.9.34, on Windows gate machines too (internal or USB webcam). The verified screen shows the Aadhaar photo, the registration photo, and the gate photo side by side.
  • Hands-free auto scan — flip the switch on the gate screen and the scanner stays armed: a worker just places a finger and the mark happens automatically (IN or OUT by their last state), with the verified card showing and clearing itself. A 90-second per-worker cooldown prevents double-marks.
  • In-app Aadhaar tools — a "Get PDF from UIDAI" button opens the official myAadhaar download page right inside registration, and the worker's phone number auto-fills from the e-Aadhaar PDF (a manually typed number is never overwritten).
  • Zero-setup Windows install — the fingerprint-scanner runtime ships inside the Windows app: unzip, plug the scanner in, scan. Nothing to install on gate machines. Android is just as simple — connect the scanner over USB and tap Allow.
  • Auto-sync every 90 seconds — while online, devices quietly pull new approvals, fingerprints, and photos, and push their marks. Nobody has to press a sync button.

Which fingerprint scanners work

ScannerWhere it stands
SecuGen HP20 Fully supported on Android and Windows — zero setup on both.
Mantra MFS100 / MFS500 Driver built in (Android) — capture and matching through Mantra's own kit, with templates that match across brands, so a worker enrolled on one scanner verifies on the other. Activates once the Mantra SDK ships with the app.
Aadhaar L1 devices (e.g. Mantra MFS110) Never usable for attendance — see the warning below.
Other brands (Morpho / IDEMIA, Startek, Tatvik, Precision…) Detected & named — the app's Diagnostics screen identifies each attached device and states the next step.
Buying scanners? Never buy an L1 device for attendance. Aadhaar L1 scanners (such as the Mantra MFS110) encrypt every fingerprint inside the sensor itself — a UIDAI design requirement for Aadhaar authentication. No attendance app can read them, TrueCrew included, and no software update can change that. When in doubt, plug the device in: the Diagnostics screen explains exactly where it stands.

13. FAQ / Troubleshooting

Someone forgot their password. What do they do?
They can reset it themselves. On the sign-in page, click Forgot password?, enter the account email, and follow the reset link that is sent. Admins can also set a new password for their own users from the Users page — and you can do it for anyone.
A user says login is blocked or "too many attempts".
Sign-in allows 5 attempts per minute from the same place. This slows down password-guessing. Wait a minute and try again — carefully. If the password is truly lost, use the Forgot password link instead of retrying.
Where do Aadhaar PDFs and ID documents live? Are they public?
No. All documents are kept in private storage — there are no public web links to them. A document can only be downloaded by a signed-in user whose role allows it: the vendor that owns the worker, or a company the worker is associated with. Every download goes through this permission check.
Why can't a vendor delete or deactivate a worker?
The engagement lock. Once a worker is deployed to a company — or is currently checked IN — the vendor cannot delete them, deactivate them, or remove their fingerprint. The deployment must be cancelled first (and the worker must be checked OUT). This protects companies from losing a worker mid-engagement. As Super Admin, you can override the lock when there is a genuine reason. Deleting a worker never deletes their attendance history.
How do the usage counters on Subscriptions work?
Users and company links count what exists. Workers is fairer: a worker counts against the plan only once they have actually worked — deployed and marked IN at least once. Registering and CSV-importing workers is free. The quota check happens at first deployment, and deleting a worker who already worked does not free the slot.
Can I change an organisation's plan without an upgrade request?
Yes. Open Subscriptions, pick the organisation, and set any plan directly. Requests exist so organisations can ask — you are never limited to them.
How do we test with duplicate Aadhaar numbers?
In production, the same Aadhaar is always rejected across all vendors — that is the point of the check. Test environments may disable the duplicate check so demo workers can share a number. Ask the technical team whether your test server has it disabled.
Which fingerprint scanners should organisations buy?
SecuGen HP20 is fully supported on Android and Windows with zero setup. Mantra MFS100 / MFS500 have a built-in driver (Android) that activates once the Mantra SDK ships with the app. Never buy Aadhaar L1 devices (such as the Mantra MFS110) for attendance — UIDAI's design encrypts fingerprints inside the sensor, so no attendance app can read them, and no update can change that. When in doubt, plug the scanner in: the app's Diagnostics screen explains exactly where each attached device stands.
What does a photo-mismatch flag on a mark mean?
Every gate capture is automatically compared with the worker's enrolled face. When they do not match, the day detail flags the capture. The flag is advisory — the mark still stands — but it is the buddy-punching alarm: someone other than the enrolled worker may have been at the camera. Open the day detail, compare the photos, and follow up with the gate.
Do visitor approvals need WhatsApp?
No. Where the WhatsApp Business connection is configured, hosts approve gate passes by replying YES or NO on WhatsApp. Without it, the gate records the host's decision manually — with a mandatory note — and every decision is audited either way. Entry is only ever allowed after an approval is on record.
