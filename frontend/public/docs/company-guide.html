How TrueCrew works for your company

TrueCrew tracks contract workers at your site — who came, when, through which gate, and for how long. Your contractors do the paperwork. You stay in control.

  1. Contractors register their workers

    Each worker is registered with a verified Aadhaar identity and an enrolled fingerprint. The contractor does this — not you.

  2. You approve access

    You decide which contractors may work with your company. By default, every single deployment then waits for your approval too — and you choose which gates each worker may use.

  3. Workers scan at your gates

    At the gate, the worker puts a thumb on the scanner — or shows their face to the camera. The device verifies them and records IN or OUT. It works even without internet.

  4. You see everything, live

    A Live Board of who is inside right now, daily attendance summaries with gate photos, per-worker analytics, CSV exports, and printable month reports. Even your visitors are tracked — photo included.

The simple split Contractors own worker registration. Your company owns the gates, the approvals, and the attendance record. Nobody can change what happened at your gate — not even the contractor.

Your three roles

Your company can have three kinds of logins. Give each person only the role they need.

RoleWho it is forWhat they can do
Company Admin The site or HR manager who runs TrueCrew for your company Everything below, plus: create users, approve contractors, change company settings, and manage the plan and billing.
Company HR The person who decides which workers may enter, and where Approves or rejects contractor deployments, chooses each worker's allowed departments and gates, and records manual OUTs. Nothing else — no users, no settings, no billing.
Gate user The security guard or attendant at an entry point Marks attendance only. Can be locked to one named gate — then they see and record only that gate.

Getting started

  1. Get your company account

    Sign up at /signup as a Company — you start on the free Trial plan right away. Or the platform admin creates your company and sends you a login.

  2. Create Gate users

    Go to Users and add one gate login per entry point. Set a gate or location name (for example "Main Gate") to lock that login to one gate — every mark it records is tagged with that gate, and it sees only that gate's records.

  3. Create HR users

    Also on the Users page. HR users handle deployment approvals and manual OUTs — nothing more. Handy when approvals should not wait for the admin.

  4. Create your own contractors (optional)

    If you bring your own contractor, create the contractor yourself — it is auto-approved for your company. You can also create that contractor's admin login and hand it over to them.

  5. Approve external contractors

    Contractors who signed up on their own send you an access request. Open Contractor Approvals — it has Pending / Approved / Rejected / Suspended tabs. Approve, or reject with a reason (the contractor sees the reason). You can suspend an approved contractor at any time, and re-approve them later.

Know your contractors

Click any contractor on your Contractors page to open a full contractor detail view — the contractor's complete story with your company, in three tabs:

  • Overview — the contractor's organisation profile (contacts, GST/PAN) and your relationship timeline: when they requested access, when you approved, when they consented to share details.
  • Workers & Deployments — every worker the contractor has deployed to you, past and present, with dates and approval status.
  • Attendance — the contractor's attendance history at your site: man-days, who is currently inside, and daily activity.
Available with the contractor's consent Contractors give this consent when they send you an access request ("the company may view our profile and track our working history"). Contractors you created yourself consent automatically. Without consent you see only the contractor's name and request status.

Departments & deployment approvals

Deployment approval is ON by default for your company. When a contractor deploys a worker to your site, the deployment waits for your HR or admin to approve it — and to choose the worker's departments and gates — before the worker can enter. Prefer to let approved contractors deploy freely? Switch off Require deployment approval with the toggle on the approvals panel.

Preset departments and gates

When you approve a worker, you choose where they may go. TrueCrew ships with a standard list — and you can always add your own names:

The approval flow

  1. A contractor deploys a worker to your company

    With a start and end date.

  2. The worker appears in your approvals panel

    On the Deploy Workers page, with timestamps showing when the deployment was requested and when it was decided. The panel also tells you clearly when auto-approval is on and nothing will queue.

  3. HR or admin selects workers

    One at a time, or many at once — on the portal's approvals panel, or in the app's Approvals tab.

  4. Pick the allowed gates and departments

    Main Gate is pre-selected, so workers can always use the main entrance. Add more departments per worker, or tap the All gates chip to remove the restriction completely.

  5. Approve — or Reject with a reason

    The contractor sees your decision and the reason. Rejected workers never appear at any gate.

No extra setup after approval Approved workers can immediately mark attendance at their allowed gates, from any gate device. The rule is enforced everywhere — on every device, online and offline.

Marking attendance at the gate

Gate staff use the TrueCrew app — on an Android phone with a USB fingerprint scanner, or on a Windows PC. Both work the same way, and both are plug-and-play: install the app, plug the scanner in, and scan. There is no scanner software to install on any machine — on Windows, the USB driver installs itself from Windows Update the first time the scanner is plugged in.

  1. The worker puts a thumb on the scanner

    No card, no name search, no typing.

  2. The app finds the match on the device itself

    It compares the scan against the fingerprints already synced to the device — so it works with no internet at all.

  3. A verified card appears

    The worker's photo, name, a greeting, and a clear IN or OUT with the time. The guard just waves them through.

  4. A proof photo is taken automatically

    The moment the thumb verifies, the device camera quietly photographs the worker and attaches the photo to that record — tamper-evident proof of who actually scanned. Works on Android phones and on Windows laptops and desktops alike (built-in or USB webcam).

  5. Everything syncs by itself

    Marks flow to the portal automatically, about every 90 seconds while the device is online. Newly approved workers reach every gate device the same way — nobody has to press anything.

Hands-free mode for busy gates Flip the auto scan switch on the gate screen and the scanner stays armed: a worker simply places a finger and the mark happens by itself — verification, photo, IN/OUT — with no taps from the guard. A 90-second per-worker cooldown prevents accidental double-marks. Workers may have two enrolled fingers (primary + backup) — either one works at the gate, so a bandaged finger is never a blocker.

Photo-verified attendance

Every mark at your gate carries visual proof — so you never have to wonder who really scanned.

  • A live photo on every row. The Attendance Log shows each worker's gate photo for the day right on the row (falling back to the registration photo).
  • Click a row for the day detail — the registration photo, the Aadhaar photo, and the gate IN and OUT captures side by side, with times, gates, how each mark was verified, and the match scores.
  • Automatic face cross-check. Every gate capture is compared against the worker's enrolled face in the background. A capture that does not match is flagged in the day detail — catching buddy-punching (one worker scanning for another). The flag is advisory: it never blocks a mark, it shows you where to look.
  • Verified marks on worker analytics. Open any worker's page: each recent mark shows its photo and how it was verified — thumb (fingerprint + score), photo (face + score), or manual.

Manual OUT & exceptions

Workers sometimes leave without scanning OUT. TrueCrew makes these cases easy to spot and easy to close.

  • The Exceptions page lists everyone currently IN — workers who scanned in but have not scanned out yet. Check it at the end of each shift.
  • HR or admin can record a manual OUT, with a reason — on the portal, or straight from the app's Inside tab. Contractors cannot — the record of who was inside your site belongs to your company. Every manual OUT is logged with who recorded it.
  • A daily missing-OUT digest goes out at 21:00 IST — as an in-app notification, and by email on plans that include email alerts — so forgotten check-outs never wait until month-end.

A day the gate missed NEW

Scanners fail and people forget to scan out. Attendance Log → Add a missed day records the day by hand — today or any date in the past.

  • It counts like any other day for attendance and wages, but is always labelled a manual entry, so your register never passes it off as a scan.
  • Your contractors can raise one too, and it waits for you. Nothing is added to the attendance record, and nothing is paid, until you approve it. Admin and HR both decide.
  • The reason is required and kept permanently — it is the evidence for a day nobody scanned.
  • A manual day is refused if the worker had no approved deployment on that date, or if the day already has attendance. If the gate records the day while a request is waiting, the request is declined rather than counted twice.

Live Board — your site right now

The Live Board answers one question at a glance: who is where, right now? It refreshes itself every few seconds — put it on a wall screen in the security office.

  • Inside now — how many workers are on site this moment, with occupancy shown against how many were expected today.
  • Per gate and department — a card for each occupied gate with the workers' photos, and a strip of the empty ones.
  • Hourly flow — today's IN and OUT movement, hour by hour.
  • Live activity feed — the most recent scans as they happen.
Everyone gets their own view Every role sees the Live Board. Your company sees the whole site; a gate login sees only its own gate; contractors see only their own workers. Night shifts are handled correctly — a worker who scanned in before midnight stays on the board until they scan out.

Visitors & gate passes

TrueCrew now manages your visitors too — guests, delivery people, interviewees — with the same discipline as worker attendance: nobody enters until someone inside says yes.

Step 1 — HR maintains the host list

On the portal's Visitors page, the Hosts tab holds the people who may receive visitors: name, mobile number, position, and department. Only HR and admins can edit this list — the gate can never invent a host.

Step 2 — the gate creates a pass

  1. A visitor arrives at the gate

    The gate team opens the app's Visitors tab and creates a gate pass in seconds: guest name, phone number, a live photo, and whom they came to meet — picked from your host list.

  2. The host is asked

    Where WhatsApp is enabled, the host gets the request on WhatsApp and simply replies YES or NO. Otherwise, the gate phones the host and records the answer as a manual decision with a mandatory note. Either way, the decision and who made it are on record.

  3. Entry only after approval

    The pass moves waiting → approved (or denied) → inside → left. A visitor cannot be let in on a pending or denied pass — the app will not record an entry.

  4. Everything lands on the portal

    The Visitors page shows today's passes with the guest photo, status, the decision trail (WhatsApp reply or manual note), and entry and exit times.

An honest visitor record Who came, who they met, who approved it, when they entered, when they left — with a photo. Every manual decision requires a note and is audited.

Payroll & wages NEW

Attendance is only half the job — what it is worth is the other half. Payroll turns the attendance you already trust into a wage register, with no re-keying.

PageWhat it is for
Wage Register The payout sheet for a pay period: days present, payable days, overtime hours, gross, deductions and net per worker — plus a contractor-wise summary for verifying a bill, and a muster in the familiar P / A / WO / H grid.
Workers & Wages What each worker is paid. Set a rate for one worker or a whole crew, filtered to one contractor or several.
Payroll Settings The year's government holidays, your weekly off, and overtime multipliers per skill grade.

Rates are paid per day

This is built for daily-wage and contract labour, so the day rate is the default: a worker earns it for each day present. Staff paid a monthly salary can be switched to per month, and their day rate becomes the monthly figure ÷ 26.

A wage change is agreed, not imposed You and the contractor can both set a rate. Because you pay it, a rate a contractor proposes for a worker deployed to you waits for your approval — the agreed rate keeps applying until you decide. The proposal, the contractor's note and your decision are all visible to both sides, and every decision is audited. Workers not deployed anywhere are priced by their contractor alone.
  • Approving a change — open Payroll → Workers & Wages. Pending proposals sit at the top of the page showing the current rate, the proposed rate and the reason. Approve or Reject; a rejection can carry your reason, and the contractor is told either way.
  • Setting a rate yourself — type it in the Rate column and save. The contractor is notified with the old and new figures, so nobody discovers a change at payout.
  • Only a company admin approves. HR can run the register and read the rates, but cannot agree a new one.

Government holidays

  • Add the year's holidays under Payroll → Settings. They are marked automatically on the attendance log and the muster.
  • A holiday is paid at the day rate, whether or not anyone works.
  • Anyone who does work a holiday earns overtime for the whole day, at the holiday multiplier.

Deductions and heads

Wages are split into the heads an Indian manufacturing wage sheet uses — Basic, DA/VDA, HRA, conveyance, washing, medical, night shift, incentive and special allowance — and PF, ESI, professional tax and LWF are computed from them, with bonus and gratuity provisions shown so you can check a contractor's bill line by line.

Confirm your state's rates before the first live payout Professional tax and Labour Welfare Fund differ by state; the defaults follow Maharashtra. PF and ESI rates and ceilings are set centrally and are configurable. Have your accountant confirm the figures for your state once, and they stay set.

Worker records & documents

  • You see the workers that concern you — everyone currently deployed to your site, plus past workers who attended before. The Workers page opens on the current deployment view, with filters like "Inside now" and "Present today".
  • You can download documents — the Aadhaar PDF and other ID documents of any worker associated with your company. Documents are stored privately, and every download is logged.
  • Verification badges — each worker's detail page shows what has been verified: Aadhaar, fingerprint, face photo, and email and phone (which your team or the contractor can attest manually until OTP checks arrive).

Rules that protect you

TrueCrew enforces a few hard rules so your attendance record always stays truthful:

  • A contractor cannot remove a worker who is on your site. While a worker is deployed to you — or still checked IN — the contractor cannot delete, deactivate, or de-register them. The deployment must be cancelled first, and your team must mark the worker OUT.
  • Deployments lock after the first attendance. Once a worker has scanned at your gate, that deployment cannot be quietly removed or rewritten.
  • A locked deployment can only be cancelled once the worker is OUT. Nobody can cancel a deployment while the worker is still inside your site.
And everything is audited Every sensitive action — approvals, manual OUTs, document downloads, deployment changes — is recorded with who did it and when.

Plan & billing

  • Live usage meters. The Plan & Billing page shows exactly where you stand against your plan's limits: users, workers, and contractor links.
  • Fair counting. A worker only counts against your plan once they have actually worked — deployed and marked their first attendance. Registering or importing workers is free, so contractors can prepare their crew without touching your quota.
  • Easy upgrades. Hitting a limit never breaks anything — the action is simply blocked with a clear message. Send an upgrade request from the billing page; the platform team confirms it. Offline payment is supported — no card needed.

FAQ / Troubleshooting

The fingerprint scanner is not detected.
Open the app's Diagnostics page — it shows the exact status of the scanner, the server connection, and sync. On Android: plug the scanner into the phone (USB-OTG) and tap Allow when asked. The permission is remembered, and plugging in again offers to open TrueCrew automatically. On Windows: nothing to install at all — the scanner libraries ship inside the app, and the USB driver installs itself from Windows Update the first time you plug the scanner in. If anything is still off, Diagnostics tells you exactly what to fix.
Can we use an Aadhaar (L1) fingerprint device?
No — and that is a UIDAI rule, not a TrueCrew limitation. L1 devices such as the Mantra MFS110 encrypt every fingerprint inside the sensor for Aadhaar authentication only, so no attendance app can read them. Use a standard scanner instead: the SecuGen HP20 is fully supported, and Mantra MFS100-class devices are supported too. The app's Diagnostics page tells you what any plugged-in device can do.
A worker is not found at the gate.
Check three things: 1) the deployment is approved — pending or rejected workers never appear at any gate; 2) this gate is in the worker's allowed gates — a worker restricted to Production will not match at Dispatch; 3) today is inside the deployment's start and end dates. Also make sure the device has synced recently — if fingerprints have not reached the device yet, the app says "Sync first, then rescan". Devices sync on their own about every 90 seconds while online.
Why can't I edit a contractor's workers?
You can view workers and download their documents, but registration belongs to the contractor — they registered the worker and hold the consent. If a name, photo, or fingerprint needs correcting, ask the contractor to update it. What you control is access: approvals, allowed gates, and the attendance record itself.
I forgot my password.
Use Forgot password on the sign-in page. Enter your email, open the reset link you receive, and set a new password. For safety, all existing sessions are signed out after a reset. This works for every role — no need to call anyone.
Where do I see notifications?
Click the bell in the portal header — approvals, deployment requests, plan decisions, and daily missing-OUT alerts all land there, with an unread badge. The app has the same bell with the same feed. In-app notifications are included on every plan; email notifications are included on Professional and above.
How does gate scoping work?
Give a gate login a location name (for example "Back Gate") and it is locked to that gate: every mark it records carries that gate's name, and it sees only that gate's records — in the attendance log, the exceptions list, and exports. Leave the location empty and the login covers all gates. Create one gate login per entry point so your records always show where each scan happened.
A worker left without scanning OUT. What now?
Open Attendance → Exceptions — it lists everyone still marked IN. An HR user or admin can record a manual OUT with a reason; the action is logged with their name. You will also get a daily digest of missing OUTs at 21:00 IST, so nothing slips through.
Can I temporarily stop a contractor?
Yes. On Contractor Approvals → Approved, click Suspend. Their workers stop matching at your gates immediately. You can re-approve the contractor at any time — no new request needed. Use Reject (with a reason) only when you want to formally decline a contractor's request.
