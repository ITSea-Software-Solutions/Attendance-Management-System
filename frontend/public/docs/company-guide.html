How TrueCrew works for your company

TrueCrew tracks contract workers at your site — who came, when, through which gate, and for how long. Your vendors do the paperwork. You stay in control.

  1. Vendors register their workers

    Each worker is registered with a verified Aadhaar identity and an enrolled fingerprint. The vendor does this — not you.

  2. You approve access

    You decide which vendors may work with your company. If you want, you can also approve every single deployment and choose which gates each worker may use.

  3. Workers scan at your gates

    At the gate, the worker puts a thumb on the scanner — or shows their face to the camera. The device verifies them and records IN or OUT. It works even without internet.

  4. You see everything, live

    Daily attendance summaries, who is inside right now, per-worker analytics, CSV exports, and printable month reports.

The simple split Vendors own worker registration. Your company owns the gates, the approvals, and the attendance record. Nobody can change what happened at your gate — not even the vendor.

Your three roles

Your company can have three kinds of logins. Give each person only the role they need.

RoleWho it is forWhat they can do
Company Admin The site or HR manager who runs TrueCrew for your company Everything below, plus: create users, approve vendors, change company settings, and manage the plan and billing.
Company HR The person who decides which workers may enter, and where Approves or rejects vendor deployments, chooses each worker's allowed departments and gates, and records manual OUTs. Nothing else — no users, no settings, no billing.
Gate user The security guard or attendant at an entry point Marks attendance only. Can be locked to one named gate — then they see and record only that gate.

Getting started

  1. Get your company account

    Sign up at /signup as a Company — you start on the free Trial plan right away. Or the platform admin creates your company and sends you a login.

  2. Create Gate users

    Go to Users and add one gate login per entry point. Set a gate or location name (for example "Main Gate") to lock that login to one gate — every mark it records is tagged with that gate, and it sees only that gate's records.

  3. Create HR users

    Also on the Users page. HR users handle deployment approvals and manual OUTs — nothing more. Handy when approvals should not wait for the admin.

  4. Create your own vendors (optional)

    If you bring your own contractor, create the vendor yourself — it is auto-approved for your company. You can also create that vendor's admin login and hand it over to them.

  5. Approve external vendors

    Vendors who signed up on their own send you an access request. Open Vendor Approvals — it has Pending / Approved / Rejected / Suspended tabs. Approve, or reject with a reason (the vendor sees the reason). You can suspend an approved vendor at any time, and re-approve them later.

Departments & deployment approvals

By default, an approved vendor can deploy workers to your site and they can start marking attendance right away. If you want more control, turn on Require deployment approval in your company settings — then every deployment waits for your yes before the worker can enter.

Preset departments and gates

When you approve a worker, you choose where they may go. TrueCrew ships with a standard list — and you can always add your own names:

The approval flow

  1. A vendor deploys a worker to your company

    With a start and end date.

  2. The worker appears in your approvals panel

    On the Deploy Workers page, with timestamps showing when the deployment was requested and when it was decided. The panel also tells you clearly when auto-approval is on and nothing will queue.

  3. HR or admin selects workers

    One at a time, or many at once.

  4. Pick the allowed gates and departments

    Main Gate is pre-selected, so workers can always use the main entrance. Add more departments per worker, or tap the All gates chip to remove the restriction completely.

  5. Approve — or Reject with a reason

    The vendor sees your decision and the reason. Rejected workers never appear at any gate.

No extra setup after approval Approved workers can immediately mark attendance at their allowed gates, from any gate device. The rule is enforced everywhere — on every device, online and offline.

Marking attendance at the gate

Gate staff use the TrueCrew app — on an Android phone with a USB fingerprint scanner, or on a Windows PC. Both work the same way.

  1. The worker puts a thumb on the scanner

    No card, no name search, no typing.

  2. The app finds the match on the device itself

    It compares the scan against the fingerprints already synced to the device — so it works with no internet at all.

  3. A verified card appears

    The worker's photo, name, a greeting, and a clear IN or OUT with the time. The guard just waves them through.

  4. A proof photo is taken automatically

    The moment the thumb verifies, the device camera quietly photographs the worker and attaches the photo to that record — tamper-evident proof of who actually scanned.

  5. Everything syncs by itself

    Marks flow to the portal automatically, about every 90 seconds while the device is online. Newly approved workers reach every gate device the same way — nobody has to press anything.

Face attendance as an alternative If a thumb will not read (wet, injured, worn), the app's Face tab verifies the worker by camera instead. Every face mark is re-checked on the server too.
Per-gate scoping A gate login with a location name is locked to that gate: it records marks under that gate's name and sees only that gate's attendance. Perfect for sites with several entrances.

Manual OUT & exceptions

Workers sometimes leave without scanning OUT. TrueCrew makes these cases easy to spot and easy to close.

  • The Exceptions page lists everyone currently IN — workers who scanned in but have not scanned out yet. Check it at the end of each shift.
  • HR or admin can record a manual OUT, with a reason. Vendors cannot — the record of who was inside your site belongs to your company. Every manual OUT is logged with who recorded it.
  • A daily missing-OUT digest goes out at 21:00 IST — as an in-app notification, and by email on plans that include email alerts — so forgotten check-outs never wait until month-end.

Worker records & documents

  • You see the workers that concern you — everyone currently deployed to your site, plus past workers who attended before. The Workers page opens on the current deployment view, with filters like "Inside now" and "Present today".
  • You can download documents — the Aadhaar PDF and other ID documents of any worker associated with your company. Documents are stored privately, and every download is logged.
  • Verification badges — each worker's detail page shows what has been verified: Aadhaar, fingerprint, face photo, and email and phone (which your team or the vendor can attest manually until OTP checks arrive).

Rules that protect you

TrueCrew enforces a few hard rules so your attendance record always stays truthful:

  • A vendor cannot remove a worker who is on your site. While a worker is deployed to you — or still checked IN — the vendor cannot delete, deactivate, or de-register them. The deployment must be cancelled first, and your team must mark the worker OUT.
  • Deployments lock after the first attendance. Once a worker has scanned at your gate, that deployment cannot be quietly removed or rewritten.
  • A locked deployment can only be cancelled once the worker is OUT. Nobody can cancel a deployment while the worker is still inside your site.
And everything is audited Every sensitive action — approvals, manual OUTs, document downloads, deployment changes — is recorded with who did it and when.

Plan & billing

  • Live usage meters. The Plan & Billing page shows exactly where you stand against your plan's limits: users, workers, and vendor links.
  • Fair counting. A worker only counts against your plan once they have actually worked — deployed and marked their first attendance. Registering or importing workers is free, so vendors can prepare their crew without touching your quota.
  • Easy upgrades. Hitting a limit never breaks anything — the action is simply blocked with a clear message. Send an upgrade request from the billing page; the platform team confirms it. Offline payment is supported — no card needed.

FAQ / Troubleshooting

The fingerprint scanner is not detected.
Open the app's Diagnostics page — it shows the exact status of the scanner, the server connection, and sync. On Android: plug the scanner into the phone (USB-OTG) and tap Allow when asked. The permission is remembered, and plugging in again offers to open TrueCrew automatically. On Windows: the app finds the SecuGen driver on its own — nothing to install by hand. If the driver is missing or the wrong type, Diagnostics tells you exactly what to fix.
A worker is not found at the gate.
Check three things: 1) the deployment is approved — pending or rejected workers never appear at any gate; 2) this gate is in the worker's allowed gates — a worker restricted to Production will not match at Dispatch; 3) today is inside the deployment's start and end dates. Also make sure the device has synced recently — if fingerprints have not reached the device yet, the app says "Sync first, then rescan". Devices sync on their own about every 90 seconds while online.
Why can't I edit a vendor's workers?
You can view workers and download their documents, but registration belongs to the vendor — they registered the worker and hold the consent. If a name, photo, or fingerprint needs correcting, ask the vendor to update it. What you control is access: approvals, allowed gates, and the attendance record itself.
I forgot my password.
Use Forgot password on the sign-in page. Enter your email, open the reset link you receive, and set a new password. For safety, all existing sessions are signed out after a reset. This works for every role — no need to call anyone.
Where do I see notifications?
Click the bell in the portal header — approvals, deployment requests, plan decisions, and daily missing-OUT alerts all land there, with an unread badge. The app has the same bell with the same feed. In-app notifications are included on every plan; email notifications are included on Professional and above.
How does gate scoping work?
Give a gate login a location name (for example "Back Gate") and it is locked to that gate: every mark it records carries that gate's name, and it sees only that gate's records — in the attendance log, the exceptions list, and exports. Leave the location empty and the login covers all gates. Create one gate login per entry point so your records always show where each scan happened.
A worker left without scanning OUT. What now?
Open Attendance → Exceptions — it lists everyone still marked IN. An HR user or admin can record a manual OUT with a reason; the action is logged with their name. You will also get a daily digest of missing OUTs at 21:00 IST, so nothing slips through.
Can I temporarily stop a vendor?
Yes. On Vendor Approvals → Approved, click Suspend. Their workers stop matching at your gates immediately. You can re-approve the vendor at any time — no new request needed. Use Reject (with a reason) only when you want to formally decline a vendor's request.
