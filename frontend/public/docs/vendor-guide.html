Your job in TrueCrew

You supply workers to client companies. TrueCrew helps you do three things:

  • Register workers with a verified identity — Aadhaar, live photo, and fingerprint.
  • Deploy workers to the companies you serve, with clear start and end dates.
  • Watch their attendance — who worked, where, and for how long. Live, not on paper.

Most of your work happens in the TrueCrew app — on an Android phone or a Windows PC. It works offline and syncs on its own. The web portal is there for company access requests, reports, and billing.

Getting started

  1. Get your account. Sign up at /signup on the web portal and choose Vendor. You start on the free Trial plan right away. Or, a company you work with creates your vendor account and shares the login with you.
  2. Request access to companies. On the web portal, open Company Access. Send a request to each company where your workers will be deployed.
  3. Wait for approval. The company reviews your request. You get a notification when they approve. You can only deploy workers to approved companies.
  4. Download the app. Open the Downloads page and get the app — an APK for Android, or the Windows app for a PC. Sign in with the same email and password you use on the portal.
Tip One login works everywhere — portal, Android app, and Windows app. Your data follows you.

Registering a worker (app or web)

Registration is a 5-step wizard. The app guides you through it.

  1. Get the worker's Aadhaar PDF. The worker downloads it from the UIDAI website. The PDF has a password: the first 4 letters of the name in CAPITALS + birth year. Example: Narendra, born 1955 → NARE1955.
  2. Import the PDF in the app. Name, date of birth, gender, and Aadhaar number fill in automatically. Just check them.
  3. Take a live photo. The app compares the live photo with the photo inside the Aadhaar PDF and shows a match check. It is advisory — a low score warns you, but an old Aadhaar photo never blocks the registration.
  4. Enroll the fingerprint. Use the SecuGen scanner. On Android: plug the scanner in with a USB-OTG cable and tap Allow once — the app remembers it. On Windows: the scanner is detected automatically.
  5. Review and save. The "Ready to save?" checklist ticks off name, Aadhaar, fingerprint, photos, and consent. When everything is green, save.
Works fully offline No internet at the site? No problem. Registrations queue on the device and sync automatically when you are back online — about every 90 seconds. Photos, the Aadhaar PDF, and fingerprints all upload with the sync.
No PDF? Use manual entry If the worker has no Aadhaar PDF, choose manual entry and type the 12-digit Aadhaar number. Aadhaar is required for every worker.

Duplicates are rejected. Each Aadhaar can exist only once in TrueCrew — even across different vendors. If someone already registered this worker, the registration is refused.

  • Filter chips at the top: All / Deployed / Not deployed / Expiring ≤3d. One tap each.
  • Search box — type a name to jump straight to a worker.
  • Sync status icon on every worker — see at once if a record is synced, still waiting, or hit an error. Tap it to read the details.

Deploying workers to a company

  1. Tap a worker in the app.
  2. Tap "Deploy to company".
  3. Pick the company. Only companies that approved your access appear in the list.
  4. Pick the date range — start date and end date.
  5. Send. Done. The worker can now mark attendance at that company's gates during those dates.

Company approval

Some companies require their HR to approve each deployment. In that case the deployment shows awaiting approval first, then turns approved or rejected. A worker cannot mark attendance until the deployment is approved.

The company also chooses which gates or departments the worker may use. That rule is enforced at every gate.

All deployments are visible on the web portal too, with the exact time each one was requested and decided.

Worker actions in the app NEW in v0.9.27

Open any worker to see the actions sheet:

  • Edit — fix the name, phone, date of birth, or gender.
  • Activate / Deactivate — turn the worker on or off.
  • Delete — remove the worker from your list.
The lock rule — read this before you delete Once a worker is deployed to a company — or is still checked IN at a gate — you cannot delete them, deactivate them, or remove their fingerprint. Cancel the deployment first. If the worker is still inside, the company must mark them OUT before the deployment can be cancelled. Editing details (name, phone, and so on) is always allowed.
  • Deleting keeps history. The worker's attendance records stay safely on the server for reports and audits.
  • Never-synced registrations can simply be removed from the device — nothing reached the server yet.

Notifications — no need to keep checking the portal

The bell in the app (and in the portal) tells you what changed:

  • Deployment approved — the company said yes. The worker can start.
  • Deployment rejected — with the company's reason.
  • Daily digest at 08:30 — two things every morning:
    • Workers who are ready but not deployed ("benched") — put them to work.
    • Deployments ending within 3 days — extend them before they lapse.

On the Professional plan and above, you also get these as email copies.

Tracking attendance

  • Daily summary — one row per worker per day: first IN, last OUT, total hours, and status (inside / done / incomplete).
  • Worker detail — tap any worker for full analytics: days present, total hours, missed OUTs, a month-by-month breakdown, and a per-company view when the worker serves more than one site.
  • Exceptions — everyone currently inside (marked IN, no OUT yet). Handy at the end of a shift.
  • CSV exports — download daily rows or monthly totals, ready for Excel. On Professional and above, you also get bulk worker import and export by CSV.

Plan & billing

The Billing page on the portal shows live usage meters — how much of your plan you have used.

The fairness rule Only workers who have actually worked count against your plan — a worker uses a plan slot only after they are deployed and marked IN at least once. Registering workers is free. Register as many as you like, keep your bench full, and pay only for workers who work.
  • Hitting a limit never breaks anything — the action is simply blocked with a clear message until you upgrade.
  • Deleting a worker who already worked does not free the slot.
  • Upgrading: send an upgrade request from the Billing page. Offline payment is supported — the TrueCrew team activates your new plan after contact.

FAQ / Troubleshooting

A worker row shows a sync error. What do I do?

Tap the sync icon on that row to read the reason. The most common one is a duplicate Aadhaar — someone (possibly another vendor) already registered this person. Each Aadhaar can exist only once in TrueCrew. Other errors usually clear on their own — the app retries on the next sync.

The fingerprint scanner is not detected on my Android phone

Unplug the scanner and plug it back in. Make sure you use a USB-OTG cable or adapter. When Android asks for USB permission, tap Allow — you only need to do this once. After that, plugging in the scanner can even open TrueCrew by itself.

Does the scanner work on Windows?

Yes. Install the standard SecuGen driver once — the app finds it automatically, wherever it installed. No extra software, no manual copying. The SecuGen Hamster Pro 20 (HU20) is supported. The app's Diagnostics screen shows exactly what was found.

The Aadhaar PDF will not open

The PDF is password-protected by UIDAI. The password is the first 4 letters of the worker's name in CAPITALS + their birth year. Example: Narendra, born 1955 → NARE1955. Use the name exactly as it appears on the Aadhaar.

Why is Delete (or Deactivate) blocked on a worker?

The lock rule. The worker is deployed to a company, or is still checked IN at a gate. Cancel the deployment first. If the worker is inside, ask the company to mark them OUT — then cancel, then delete. Their attendance history stays on the server either way.

The worker's photo did not upload

Photos, Aadhaar PDFs, and fingerprints upload with the automatic sync. If a photo is missing on the server, it retries on the next sync — about every 90 seconds while the app is online. Keep the app open with internet for a minute and check again.

I forgot my password

Use Forgot password? on the sign-in page of the web portal. Enter your login email and follow the link you receive to set a new password. Then sign in to the app with the new password.

A company does not appear when I try to deploy

Only companies that approved your access appear in the Deploy list. Check the Company Access page on the web portal — your request may still be pending, or it was rejected or suspended. Send the request (or ask the company to approve it) and try again once it shows approved.
