1. Who we are

TrueCrew is a workforce registration, attendance and visitor-management platform operated by [COMPANY LEGAL NAME], [REGISTERED ADDRESS], India ("we", "us"). For the purposes of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), we act as a Data Processor on behalf of the companies and vendor organisations that use TrueCrew (the "Customer" — the Data Fiduciary for their workers' and visitors' data).

3. What we deliberately do NOT do

  • We never store the full Aadhaar number — only a masked copy and an irreversible keyed hash.
  • Biometric templates are encrypted at rest and matching runs on our own infrastructure or on the gate device — biometric data is never sold or sent to third-party services.
  • We do not use worker data for advertising or profiling.

4. Consent

Workers give informed consent at registration (recorded with the registration). Vendor organisations consent to details-sharing when requesting access to a company. Visitor hosts are contacted only because the Customer added them to its visitor-host list. Consent may be withdrawn by contacting the Customer or our grievance officer; withdrawal leads to deletion as per Section 7.

5. Where data lives and who can see it

Data is stored on servers located in [SERVER REGION, e.g. India / DigitalOcean BLR]. Access is role-scoped: a company sees only workers deployed to it and its own visitors; a vendor sees only its own workers; gate users see only their gate. Every sensitive action is recorded in an audit log (user, action, time, IP).

6. Sharing

We share personal data only: (a) between a worker's vendor and the companies the vendor is approved for, as the service requires; (b) with communication providers strictly to deliver messages the Customer triggers (e.g. email provider, WhatsApp Business API for host approvals); (c) when required by law. We do not sell personal data.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Biometric templates and documents are deleted when a worker is deleted by their vendor (attendance history is retained without biometrics).
  • Attendance and visitor records are retained for [3 years] for the Customer's statutory and payroll purposes, then deleted.
  • Backups roll off within 30 days of deletion.

8. Security

HTTPS in transit; encrypted biometric storage; documents on private storage served only through authenticated, role-checked endpoints; login rate-limiting; audit logging; least-privilege roles; regular backups. No system is perfectly secure; we notify the Customer and the Data Protection Board as required by the DPDP Act in the event of a personal data breach.

9. Your rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

Data principals (workers, visitors, users) may request access, correction, or erasure of their personal data, and may nominate a representative. Requests go to the Customer (the Data Fiduciary) or to our grievance officer below; we support the Customer in fulfilling them.
