TrueCrew — Verified Workforce Attendance

Release Notes

What changed in every version. ← Back to downloads

Version naming: Apps vX.Y.Z = the Android APK and Windows app (always released together) · Platform vX.Y.Z = the web portal & server. Preview builds are pre-1.0 and may change quickly.

Apps (Android + Windows)

Apps v0.9.29 Smarter fingerprint identification 17 Aug 2026 · current

  • 1:N ambiguity guard — when two different workers score within 10 points of each other, the scan is rejected and the guard rescans instead of guessing. Protects against look-alike prints as the worker pool grows.

Apps v0.9.28 Company tools in the app 17 Aug 2026

  • Approvals tab for HR and company admins — pending vendor deployments with requested dates; select many, pick allowed gates/departments (Main Gate preselected, "All gates" chip), approve in bulk or reject with a reason. Same powers as the portal.
  • Manual OUT from the Inside tab — HR/admin can mark a worker OUT who left without scanning (vendors never see this).
  • Attendance detail sheet — tap any Activity row: all three photos (Aadhaar · registration · that mark's gate capture) plus time, gate, method, score and sync state. Camera icon marks rows that carry a gate photo.
  • Worker list rows now show the worker's photo.

Apps v0.9.27 Worker actions from the app 17 Aug 2026

  • Edit / Activate / Deactivate / Delete on the worker sheet — vendors manage workers without the portal. Edit covers name, phone, DOB, gender.
  • Engagement lock — while a worker is deployed to a company (or still checked IN) delete, deactivate and fingerprint removal are refused with a clear reason; cancel the deployment first. Editing stays allowed.
  • Registrations that never synced can be removed from the device safely.

Apps v0.9.26 Vendor app-first 17 Aug 2026

  • Deployment info on every worker row — where they're deployed and until when, color-coded: teal (deployed), amber (ends ≤3 days / awaiting approval), grey (not deployed). Works offline.
  • Filter chips — All / Deployed / Not deployed / Expiring ≤3d, computed on-device.
  • Notifications in the app — a bell with unread badge opens the same feed as the portal: approvals, rejections, benched-worker and expiring-deployment digests. Vendors no longer need the website for day-to-day work.

Apps v0.9.25 Scanner session hardening 17 Aug 2026

  • Fixed: "Capture failed (SDK code 2)" after gate matching. Old demo workers carry simulated fingerprints; feeding those into the real matcher could sour the scanner session. Simulated/invalid templates are now filtered out before matching, and the scanner session auto-recovers (re-initialises and retries once) if it ever degrades.

Apps v0.9.24 Always fresh 17 Aug 2026

  • Automatic background sync every ~90 seconds while online — newly approved deployments, fingerprints and photos reach every gate device without anyone tapping the sync chip.
  • Supports the new HR role (HR signs into the portal to approve; devices pick the results up automatically).

Apps v0.9.23 Stability + vendor fixes 17 Aug 2026

  • Fixed: crash after taking a registration photo on Windows. The camera now settles, saves, and closes in a safe order before leaving the capture screen.
  • Fixed: "no company found" when deploying from the app — the approved-companies list read the wrong field; it now matches the web portal.
  • Worker detail shows deployments — company, dates and approval status (approved / awaiting approval / rejected), offline too.
  • Aadhaar duplicate-check now follows the server's setting — test servers can allow the same Aadhaar on multiple demo workers (production keeps it enforced).
  • Automatic gate proof photos stay on Android; Windows keeps proof photos via the Face flow (desktop camera stability).

Apps v0.9.21 Gate permissions 17 Aug 2026

  • Gate devices honour the company's deployment approvals: pending/rejected workers never appear for matching, and gate-restricted workers only match at their allowed gates/departments.
  • Vendors see the approval status of each deployment; the deploy confirmation says when a deployment is waiting for company approval.

Apps v0.9.20 True offline fingerprint matching 17 Aug 2026

  • The thumb now matches automatically at the gate. Deployed workers' enrolled fingerprints sync to gate devices (marking roles only), and every scan runs a real 1:N match on the device — Windows via the SecuGen SDK, Android via USB-OTG — fully offline, same threshold as the web gate.
  • The "select worker" screen is now a clearly-labelled MANUAL OVERRIDE, used only when no match clears the threshold — recorded with score 0 and the gate photo, so overrides are auditable.
  • Clear status when templates haven't synced to the device yet ("Sync first, then rescan").

Apps v0.9.19 Desktop-friendly layout 17 Aug 2026

  • Responsive on Windows — wide windows get a proper side navigation rail (labels expand on large screens) instead of the phone-style bottom tabs, forms widen to use the space, and lists center at a readable width. Phones keep the familiar layout.

Apps v0.9.18 Gate proof photos 17 Aug 2026

  • Automatic gate photo on fingerprint verification — the moment a worker's thumb verifies, the device camera quietly photographs them; the photo saves with that IN/OUT record and uploads on sync as tamper-evident proof. Best-effort: no camera, no problem — the mark never blocks.
  • Three-photo verified screen — every IN/OUT now shows the Aadhaar document photo, the registration photo, and the just-taken gate photo side by side, with a directional IN/OUT animation (green arrow in, amber arrow out).
  • The Aadhaar photo extracted at registration now syncs to the server, so every gate device can display it.

Apps v0.9.17 Windows camera capture 17 Aug 2026

  • Fixed: "Camera unavailable (cameraDelegate)" on Windows registration. Desktop platforms have no built-in camera dialog — the app now opens its own capture screen (live preview, capture button, camera switch) using the same camera engine as the Face tab.

Apps v0.9.16 Camera photo reliability 16 Aug 2026

  • Fixed: photo taken but never appearing. Android may close the app while the camera is open; the photo is now recovered automatically when the app returns. This also fixes photos not reaching the server (nothing was saved locally to upload).
  • Visible photo feedback — a spinner in the Live photo slot while the photo processes, and any rejection ("no face detected") now shows in red right inside the Photos card.
  • If the on-device face check is unavailable on a phone, the photo is accepted and the server validates it on sync instead of failing silently.

Apps v0.9.15 Identity match at enrollment 16 Aug 2026

  • Aadhaar photo extracted automatically — importing the PDF now also pulls the worker's Aadhaar photo into the form.
  • Two-photo identity check — the Aadhaar photo and the live camera photo are compared by the face engine the moment both exist, with an animated match indicator (e.g. "match 87%"). Low scores warn the operator; old Aadhaar photos never block registration.
  • Registration checklist — a live "Ready to save?" panel ticks off name, Aadhaar, fingerprint (with quality), photos and consent as you go.

Apps v0.9.14 Field-ready gate experience 16 Aug 2026

  • Aadhaar PDF import in the app — pick the e-Aadhaar PDF during registration: name, DOB, gender and Aadhaar auto-fill, and the PDF attaches to the worker's record on sync. Masked PDFs verified by last-4 match. The whole registration now completes in one place.
  • Gate "verified" screen — after every successful scan (finger or face): animated check, the worker's photo, name, IN/OUT with time, and a greeting. Auto-clears for the next worker.
  • Vendor tools — tap a worker for live analytics (days present, hours, missed OUTs) and deploy to a company with a date range, right from the app.
  • Android fix: the USB permission dialog now appears correctly (Android 14 restriction); plugging in a scanner offers to open TrueCrew and remembers the permission.
  • Windows: Face attendance tab enabled (camera support added).

Apps v0.9.13 Scanner SDK bundled 16 Aug 2026

  • Android is plug-and-scan: the SecuGen fingerprint SDK now ships inside the APK — connect a scanner via USB-OTG, tap Allow, capture. Nothing to install on any phone.

Apps v0.9.12 Windows scanner auto-discovery 16 Aug 2026

  • Windows finds the SecuGen library automatically wherever the driver installed it — no SDK install, no manual copying, no background service. Diagnostics show exactly which library loaded.
  • Clear guidance when something is missing (including 32-bit vs 64-bit mismatches).

Apps v0.9.11 Full registration + on-device biometrics 16 Aug 2026

  • Complete worker registration in-app: details, consent, fingerprint, photo — offline-capable, syncs when connected. Photo auto-enrolls the worker for camera (face) attendance.
  • Windows talks to the scanner directly through its SDK (the old browser-service requirement is gone). Android USB scanner channel + on-device face quality checks (real face, eyes open) before any photo is accepted.
  • In-app camera Face attendance tab (Android); Diagnostics screen with device connect/retry and precise status for server, scanner and sync.
  • Password show/hide toggles on all sign-in screens.

Apps v0.9.0–0.9.7 First releases 16 Aug 2026

  • Android + Windows apps: sign in, role-based screens (vendor / gate / admin view), offline store, automatic two-way sync with duplicate-proof records, simulated + real capture paths, downloads page distribution and automated builds.

Web platform

Platform v1.8.0 Vendor detail view + consent 18 Aug 2026 · current

  • Tab-based vendor detail for companies — click any vendor: organisation profile (contacts, GST/PAN), relationship timeline (requested / approved / consent), workers & deployments history with you, and attendance history (man-days, currently inside, daily activity).
  • Details-sharing consent — vendors now give explicit consent when requesting company access ("the company may view our profile and track our working history"); company-created vendors consent implicitly. Without consent the company sees only the vendor's name and request status.

Platform v1.7.2 Biometric hardening 17 Aug 2026

  • Photo identity cross-check — every gate capture is automatically compared to the worker's enrolled face (advisory); the day detail flags captures that don't match. Catches buddy-punching.
  • 1:N ambiguity margins — fingerprint (10 pts) and face (0.08) identifications are rejected when two workers score too close together; face encoder now rejects blurry/tiny faces instead of producing unreliable matches.
  • Removed the development byte-similarity fingerprint matcher — server-side fingerprint verification now requires a real matcher binary and refuses clearly otherwise (the gate apps match on-device and are unaffected). Enrollment quality floor 30/100.
  • Developer Guide gains a full Biometric Matching Internals chapter: exact models, formats, score scales, thresholds, and roadmap.

Platform v1.7.1 Verified marks on worker analytics 17 Aug 2026

  • Worker analytics page: every recent mark now shows its gate capture photo and HOW it was verified — thumb (fingerprint + score), photo (face + score), or manual.

Platform v1.7.0 Approval by default + photo-verified logs 17 Aug 2026

  • Deployment approval is now ON by default — every vendor deployment waits for HR/admin review (with gate/department selection) unless the company turns it off in the approvals panel. The toggle now loads its real saved value.
  • Attendance log shows the live gate photo on every row (falls back to the registration photo). Click a row for the day detail: registration + Aadhaar + gate IN/OUT captures side by side, with times, duration, events, gates, method and match scores.
  • Daily summary API now carries proof-photo ids, methods and best scores.

Platform v1.6.1 Worker engagement lock 17 Aug 2026

  • Once a worker is engaged with a company — deployed, or currently checked IN — the vendor cannot delete, deactivate, or remove the fingerprint. The API refuses with the company name and end date; cancel the deployment first. Editing details stays allowed.
  • Activate/deactivate now require admin roles (gate users can no longer change worker status); only the owning vendor can remove a fingerprint.
  • Docs reorganised into role guides: Super Admin, Company, Vendor — plus the refreshed technical Developer Guide.

Platform v1.6.0 Deployment awareness 17 Aug 2026

  • Daily vendor digests — workers ready but not deployed, and deployments ending within 3 days (in-app for all plans; email on Professional+).
  • Deployment-state filter on the Workers page: Deployed now / Not deployed / Expiring in 3 days.
  • Timestamps where they matter — user accounts show when they were added; deployments show when they were requested and decided.

Platform v1.5.1 Worker lifecycle & fair plan counting 17 Aug 2026

  • Delete worker — vendors can delete their workers (attendance history is preserved). Deletion is blocked while the worker is checked IN.
  • Fair plan counting — a worker consumes plan quota only once they have ACTUALLY WORKED (deployed + first attendance). Registering, importing and deleting workers is free; the quota check happens at first deployment. Deleting a worked worker does not free the slot.
  • Manual OUT (company/HR only) — admins and HR can administratively mark a worker OUT (forgotten check-outs, before cancelling a deployment). Vendors deliberately cannot — gate truth belongs to the company. Every manual OUT is audited with who did it.

Platform v1.5.0 Department HR role & preset departments 17 Aug 2026

  • New role: Department HR — company admins create HR logins (Users page) who approve/reject vendor deployments and choose which departments each worker may enter. HR cannot manage users, settings or billing.
  • Preset departments — a standard list (Main Gate, Security, HR, Production, Quality, Stores & Warehouse, Maintenance, Dispatch, Canteen, Admin Office…) appears everywhere departments are picked; custom names still allowed.
  • Main Gate preselected when approving — workers can always IN/OUT at the main gate by default; HR adds more departments per worker. An "All gates" option clears the restriction.
  • The approvals panel now explains itself: it says clearly when auto-approval is on and nothing will queue.

Platform v1.4.0 Deployment approvals & gate permissions 17 Aug 2026

  • Company-controlled deployment approval — HR/managers can require approval of vendor deployments (toggle on the Deployment Approvals page). Pending workers cannot mark attendance anywhere until approved; rejections carry a reason back to the vendor.
  • Per-gate / per-department permissions — approve one or many workers at once and optionally restrict them to specific gates. The rule is enforced everywhere: web gate, both apps (online and offline), and on the server for every synced mark.
  • Notifications on both sides: company admins get approval requests; vendors get the decision.

Platform v1.3.1 In-portal release notes & richer plans 16 Aug 2026
