Release Notes

What changed in every version. ← Back to downloads

Version naming: Apps vX.Y.Z = the Android APK and Windows app (always released together) · Platform vX.Y.Z = the web portal & server. Preview builds are pre-1.0 and may change quickly.

Apps (Android + Windows)

Apps v0.9.37 Third finger & visitor vehicles 31 Aug 2026

  • A third finger per worker — heavy manual work wears prints down, so a third finger can be enrolled during registration or added later from the worker sheet. Any of the three opens the gate.
  • Vehicle on gate passes — the number plate and a second photo of the vehicle. At least one photo is required, the visitor or the vehicle, so a pass can never be raised with no visual record.

Apps v0.9.35 Backup finger 27 Aug 2026

  • Enroll TWO fingers per worker — registration now offers a backup finger (e.g. the other thumb) right after the first scan, and existing workers get an "Add backup finger" action in the worker sheet. If a finger is cut, bandaged or worn, the other still marks attendance.
  • Any enrolled finger verifies at the gate — every scanner driver (SecuGen Windows, SecuGen Android, Mantra) matches the live scan against both enrolled fingers and takes the worker's best score. Thresholds and the two-worker ambiguity guard are unchanged — a worker's own two fingers can never look like two different people.
  • Backup fingers ride the normal offline sync — captured offline at registration, pushed with the worker, and delivered to every gate device.

Apps v0.9.34 Visitor gate passes 22 Aug 2026

  • Visitors tab for gate users — create a gate pass in seconds: guest name, phone, live photo, and whom they came to meet (from the HR-maintained host list). The host is asked on WhatsApp (reply YES/NO) where configured; a phone answer is recorded as a manual decision with a mandatory note.
  • Entry only after approval — the pass tracks waiting → approved/denied → inside → left, with entry/exit times and the guest photo on the portal's new Visitors page.
  • Gate proof camera now works on Windows too — IN/OUT marks photograph the person at the gate on laptops and desktops (internal or USB webcam), not just Android.
  • Includes v0.9.33: hands-free auto scan, phone autofill from Aadhaar, background captures on Windows.

Apps v0.9.33 Hands-free gate + phone autofill 19 Aug 2026

  • Hands-free auto scan — flip the switch on the gate screen and the scanner stays armed: a worker just places a finger, the mark happens automatically (IN/OUT by last state), the verified card shows and dismisses itself. 90-second per-worker cooldown prevents double-marks; needs a real scanner (disabled in simulation).
  • Windows: fingerprint captures no longer freeze the screen — they run in the background (manual and hands-free).
  • Phone number now autofills from the Aadhaar PDF in the app, like the portal (e-Aadhaar carries the registered mobile). A manually-typed number is never overwritten.

Apps v0.9.32 UIDAI link in registration 18 Aug 2026

  • "Get PDF from UIDAI" button on the Aadhaar import step — opens the official myAadhaar download page in the browser (same link as the web wizard), so the worker's e-Aadhaar can be fetched and imported without leaving the flow.

Apps v0.9.31 Windows: true plug-and-play 18 Aug 2026

  • SecuGen runtime bundled in the Windows zip — sgfplib.dll and all companion libraries now ship beside truecrew.exe. Unzip, plug the scanner in, scan — no SecuGen software installation on gate machines. (The USB driver itself auto-installs from Windows Update on first plug.)
  • Android has been plug-and-play since v0.9.13 (SDK inside the APK, USB auto-grant) — both platforms are now zero-setup.

Apps v0.9.30 Mantra support + scanner intelligence 18 Aug 2026

  • Mantra MFS100/MFS500 driver built-in (Android) — capture and on-device matching through Mantra's own SDK, same ISO templates as SecuGen, so workers enrolled on either brand match at either gate. Activates in the official APK once the Mantra SDK is bundled.
  • Every scanner explained — Diagnostics now lists all attached USB devices and tells you exactly where you stand per brand: SecuGen ready, Mantra ready-to-activate, and Aadhaar L1 devices (e.g. MFS110) clearly marked as unusable for attendance — they encrypt fingerprints inside the sensor by UIDAI design; no app can read them.
  • Morpho/IDEMIA, Startek, Tatvik, Precision and other Indian brands are detected and named with next steps.

Apps v0.9.29 Smarter fingerprint identification 17 Aug 2026

  • 1:N ambiguity guard — when two different workers score within 10 points of each other, the scan is rejected and the guard rescans instead of guessing. Protects against look-alike prints as the worker pool grows.

Web platform

Platform v1.19.0 Manual attendance & payroll corrections 31 Aug 2026 · current

  • Record a day the gate missed — scanners fail and people forget to scan out, and the day was worked anyway. Attendance Log → Add a missed day records it by hand, for today or any past date. It counts towards attendance and wages like any other day, and is always labelled a manual entry so your register never passes it off as a scan.
  • Contractors can raise one; the company agrees it — a contractor can report a missed day for their worker, but nothing is added to the attendance record, and nothing is paid, until the company approves it. Company admin and HR can both decide. Both sides see the request, the reason, and the outcome.
  • It cannot be abused — a manual day is only accepted against an approved deployment covering that date, is refused if the day already has attendance (a duplicate would be an overpayment), and the reason is mandatory and kept permanently. If the gate records the day while a request is waiting, the request is declined rather than doubled.
  • Fixed: a wage rate that saved but changed nothing — the wage register's rate box wrote the monthly figure for every worker, including daily-wage workers, who are paid from the day rate. It said "saved" while the day rate and net payable stayed at zero. It now writes the rate the worker is actually paid from, and shows "per day" or "per month" beneath it.
  • Fixed: the Employment tab came back empty — wages, bank details, PF/ESI numbers and the head-wise split saved correctly but were never loaded back, so reopening a worker showed blank fields.
  • "Daily wage" and "Monthly salary" replace "monthly rate" through the worker form, the wage register and both exports — this product pays contract labour by the day.
  • Payroll per contractor — pick one contractor, several, or all at the top of Workers & Wages, then run the payroll for exactly that selection: open the wage register scoped to them, or pull the Excel or CSV straight down. The Wage Register gained the same picker. A company with four contractors pays four bills, and now produces four.
  • Fix a day without leaving the page — every row on Workers & Wages has an Attendance button that opens the missed-day form with that worker already filled in. Correcting a wrong day and setting a rate are the same errand.
  • Muster sheet in Excel, alongside CSV — it is the sheet that replaces the paper form.
  • Security: the spreadsheet library used to read imported Excel files was abandoned upstream and carried a prototype-pollution flaw. Replaced with the maintained official build.

Platform v1.18.1 PAN registration, wage approval & one word for contractors 31 Aug 2026

  • Register a worker with a PAN card — Aadhaar is no longer the only way in. Upload a PAN card as a photo or a PDF (e-PAN PDFs open with the date of birth as the password) and the name, father's name, date of birth and PAN number are read off it. Aadhaar works the same way from a photo now, not just the UIDAI PDF. A worker can be registered on either document; the second one can be added later.
  • Register from the web portal — registration no longer stops at the fingerprint step. A worker registered on the portal is saved as Pending and completes enrolment at the gate on the desktop or Android app, which is where the scanner is. Office staff can do the paperwork; the gate does the finger.
  • Wage changes are agreed, not imposed — the company and the contractor can both set a day rate, but the company pays, so a rate a contractor proposes for a deployed worker waits for the company to approve it. The agreed rate keeps applying meanwhile. Both sides see the proposal, the note behind it and the decision; the contractor is told the outcome and the reason for a rejection. Workers on the bench are still priced by the contractor alone.
  • When the company sets a rate, the contractor is told — with the old and the new number, so nobody discovers a change at payout.
  • One word: contractor — the portal, the apps and the guides said "contractor" in some places and "contractor" in others. It is contractor everywhere now.
  • Attendance marking removed from the web — a browser cannot reach a fingerprint scanner, so the page could never do the job. Marking lives in the desktop and Android apps, which talk to the scanner directly and work offline.
  • Fixed: the ID Document column was blank for company logins even when an Aadhaar PDF was on file.
  • Fixed: identity cards photographed on a coloured background (most real Aadhaar and PAN cards) failed to read. Digits read off a photograph are now always shown for confirmation rather than trusted silently.

Platform v1.7.2 Biometric hardening 17 Aug 2026

Platform v1.5.1 Worker lifecycle & fair plan counting 17 Aug 2026

  • Delete worker — contractors can delete their workers (attendance history is preserved). Deletion is blocked while the worker is checked IN.
