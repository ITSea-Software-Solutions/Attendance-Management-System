Biometrics the apps support

👍

Fingerprint — works offline

SecuGen Hamster Pro 20 — full support on Android and Windows. Mantra MFS100/MFS500 — driver built into the Android app (activates with the bundled Mantra SDK). Aadhaar L1 devices (e.g. Mantra MFS110, L1 Morphos) cannot share fingerprints with any app — that's a UIDAI rule; the app's Diagnostics explains this when one is plugged in. Identification runs on the device, so gates keep working with no internet.

Note: a phone's built-in fingerprint sensor can only unlock the phone — no app can use it to identify workers. That's an OS rule on every Android device, which is why gates use the USB scanner.

📷

Face — in both apps and the web portal

Uses the device camera only — no extra hardware. Workers are enrolled automatically from their registration photo; the Face tab identifies them and the server re-verifies every mark. Needs internet (identification runs server-side); fingerprint covers offline gates.

🪪

Backup options

QR ID-badges (guard sees the worker's photo on scan) and NFC tap cards are available as fallbacks. Gate operators unlock the app itself with their device's own fingerprint/face (Android biometrics, Windows Hello).
