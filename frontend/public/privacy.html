Consent

Vendors/companies registering a worker are responsible for obtaining that worker's informed consent for identity and biometric processing, as required by applicable law. AMS processes this data on the registering organisation's instructions.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or share personal data with third parties for marketing.
  • We do not use worker biometrics for anything except attendance matching.
  • We do not store full Aadhaar numbers, raw fingerprint images, or PDF passwords.

Storage & security

Data is stored on our servers with role-based access control, encrypted biometric templates, private (non-web-accessible) document storage, rate-limited authentication, and full audit trails. Offline apps keep an encrypted local copy scoped to your organisation, protected by your device sign-in; signing out wipes it.

Retention & your rights

Data is retained while your account is active. Organisations may request export or deletion of their data; workers may exercise access/correction/erasure rights through the organisation that registered them (as data fiduciary) — we support fulfilment as processor. Some records may be retained where law requires.

Changes

We'll post updates to this policy here and note material changes in the product.

Contact / Grievance

Privacy questions or grievances: contact ITSea Software Solutions via your AMS administrator. We aim to respond within 30 days.
