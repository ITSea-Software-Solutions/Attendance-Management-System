Who this policy covers — and the two roles we play

  • Account data (organisation and user details): we are the Data Fiduciary — we decide how it's processed to run your account.

Consent

Registering a worker requires the organisation to confirm — at the moment of registration, recorded with a timestamp — that the worker has given free, informed consent for processing their identity documents and biometrics for attendance purposes. Workers may withdraw consent through their organisation; withdrawal leads to removal of biometric enrollment and, where requested and lawful, erasure of their record.

What we do not do

  • No selling or sharing of personal data for advertising or marketing.
  • No use of biometrics for anything except attendance matching within your organisation's scope.
  • No storage of full Aadhaar numbers, raw fingerprint images, or e-Aadhaar PDF passwords.
  • No profiling or automated decisions with legal effect — attendance records are inputs for humans.

Where data is stored; transfers

The service is currently hosted on DigitalOcean infrastructure located in the United States. The DPDP Act permits such cross-border processing (no restricted-country notification applies as of this version). If hosting moves to an India region we will update this policy. Offline apps additionally keep an encrypted, organisation-scoped copy on the device, protected by your sign-in; signing out wipes it. Transport encryption (HTTPS) is enabled on production domains; evaluation environments may operate over HTTP and should not carry real worker data.

Your rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

Data principals (account holders and workers) have the right to access a summary of their personal data, correction and completion, erasure (subject to legal retention), grievance redressal, and to nominate a person to exercise rights on their behalf. Workers: contact the organisation that registered you (it is the Data Fiduciary for your data); we support these requests as its processor. Account holders: contact us directly.

Breach notification

If a personal-data breach affects your data, we will inform affected organisations without undue delay and support notifications to the Data Protection Board of India and affected individuals as the law requires.

Children

The service is for working adults; accounts and worker registrations are restricted to individuals 18 years or older.
